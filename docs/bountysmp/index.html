ABOUT THE PROJECT

A private, application-based survival server founded by Theoatsa, Ploopier, and FoldarFlicker. Bounty SMP turned the hunt for an assigned target into a path toward new powers.

01

Hunt. Earn. Craft.

Defeating your assigned target increased your target-kill count. Five target kills earned a shard, which could be used to craft powers.

02

An idea shaped by friends.

FoldarFlicker led the server as owner. Periklis20M joined as a helper, later became an admin, developed the plugin, and expanded its gameplay concept. JohnFG and Ofely also helped as admins.

03

A short chapter in our story.

Bounty SMP lasted around two months. Its demanding progression eventually lost the community's interest, and the server became inactive. It remains a meaningful part of what we built together.

The server's community was on Discord .
